Sylvia · Your library of thought · Design language draft 0.1 · 5 October 2026

Marginalia

A quiet page, a disciplined set of marks, and the reader's own words set brightest.

A book carries its meaning in the text and its reader's meaning in the margin. Sylvia borrows that arrangement. The ground is paper, not panels. Type does the work that gradients and cards usually do. Four kinds of mark stay visibly distinct: a quotation, a note, an audio moment and a generated answer. Status words are a fixed vocabulary, so the interface says only what is true.

Proposed for team review alongside the V1 implementation plan. Every token, component and phrase here maps to a requirement or invariant in that plan; nothing is decorative.

01

Principles

Five rules that decide every screen. When a design question comes up, one of these should settle it.

1
Paper, not panels

One warm ground, hairline rules, flat surfaces. Depth appears only on sheets and the mini-player, the two things that float above the page.

2
Ink first

Hierarchy comes from type, weight and spacing. Color is reserved for meaning: one action accent, one highlight accent, and the marker set. Never decoration.

3
A quote is not a note is not an answer

Each capture kind has its own glyph, label, typeface treatment and tint. Color never works alone, so the distinction survives colorblindness, grayscale and VoiceOver.

4
Say what is true

"Saved" only after a durable write. "Ready" is not "indexed". "Generated" is always labelled. The status vocabulary in section 06 is part of the design system, not copywriting.

5
Reachable with one thumb

Primary actions live in the lower half. Targets are 44pt or larger. Anything draggable also has a numeric control. Dynamic Type everywhere, including the reader.

02

Color

Two accents of similar lightness carry the brand: Pine for actions and the user's own notes, Ochre for highlights and exact quotations. A separate, muted marker set names the other capture kinds and statuses.

All text colors meet 4.5:1 on their ground in both modes.

Literata for everything that belongs to a book: titles, quotations and the default reading face. The system face for everything that belongs to the interface. Monospace for values a person might compare character by character.

04

Shape, space and motion

A 4pt grid, three radii and one hairline. Shadows only on the two floating objects. Motion is short, eased out and honors Reduce Motion.

Radii
10 controls · 14 cards and covers · 22 sheets · full for pills and chips
Spacing
4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 20 · 24 · 32. Screen gutter 20 on iPhone, 32 on iPad. Reading margins are the user's.
Hairline and elevation
Rules separate; cards do not nest. Shadow 0 8 24 at 10% ink, only on sheets and the mini-player.
Motion
200 ms ease-out · sheets
120 ms · highlight appears
No bounces, no parallax. Progress rules move; nothing else animates while reading.

06

Words

Status language is a component. Each moment has a fixed set of phrases tied to the state machines in the implementation plan, and a list of words the interface never uses.

A phrase changes only when the underlying state changes.
