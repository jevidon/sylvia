# Third-party inventory

Everything third-party in Sylvia's tree at M0 integration (13 October 2026; revised after review rounds 2 and 3, 14 October in New Zealand): the Swift packages and their lock, the code and assets those packages carry, Sylvia's own vendored fonts, the toolchain and host tools, the CI tooling and the fixture sources. Prepared by the M0 dependency-lock agent: **agent-reviewed; human sign-off pending.** The decisions (the lock, the license posture, the update procedure, which packages the product may use, and what needs the product owner or counsel) are in [ADR-0014](../ADRs/0014-dependency-lock-and-license-inventory.md).

- **Machine-readable source:** [`inventory.json`](inventory.json). `Scripts/check-dependency-lock.py` (CI drop-in `Scripts/ci.d/12-dependency-lock.sh`) fails when the repository and the inventory disagree, including when this page lacks a package's name, version and revision.
- **License texts:** [`texts/`](texts), copied from each package checkout at its pinned revision (digests checked in CI), or fetched or extracted where the package has none; provenance below.
- **Acknowledgements draft:** [`acknowledgements-draft.md`](acknowledgements-draft.md), generated from the inventory.
- **Evidence:** `Docs/Stage0/evidence/M0-dependencies/` (logs and the commands that wrote them), all on the host: macOS 26.6.2 (25G83), Apple M5 Max, Xcode 27.0 (27A266a), the pinned Xcode when they were written. No simulator was booted and no device was used: the iOS builds in `linkage.log` target a generic iOS Simulator destination. Since 20 October 2026 the pin is Xcode 27.1 (27A9275), the product owner's decision of PO-35 as ADR-0001 records the reading of the owner's reply; the re-pin's toolchain log, the comparison of 27.1's build-setting specifications with 27.0's (identical output) and the dependency lock check passing under the new pin are in `Docs/Stage0/evidence/M0-Integration/repin-27.1/` (the toolchain row below).

## Updating

See ADR-0014, "Update procedure". In short: change the requirement, re-resolve every lock that holds the package in the same commit, re-read its license at the new revision, survey what it vendors (`commands/vendored-headers.py` fails on an origin note or vendored-code directory not yet matched to this page), search advisories (`commands/advisories-search.py`, and `commands/vendored-advisories.py` for the code packages vendor; a new advisory fails either until it is judged and recorded), update `inventory.json` (and this page and the acknowledgements draft, which CI regenerates and compares), and run `Scripts/ci.sh dependency-lock` and `commands/run-all.sh`.
