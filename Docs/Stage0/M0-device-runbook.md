# M0 device and human-review runbook

For the product owner, to run on your own devices. It gathers every physical-device and human step that Stage 0 (M0) still needs from the eight device checklists in [`device-checklists/`](device-checklists/) and from every pending-device and pending-human row of the eight gate reports, and puts them into as few sittings as possible, in the order that unblocks the M0 gate first (plan Sections 21.1, 21.2 and 22.4).

- **Where a step here and its checklist step differ, the checklist step wins** (since 20 October 2026). This runbook was written from the checklists at `471b143`; the gates' later review branches, merged at M0 assembly, changed them afterwards. Until 19 October 2026 nine of the steps they added were missing here (M0 final review R1-01; added that day as 2.21, 3.16, 4.18, 4.19, 5.8 and 5.9), and until 20 October 2026 the steps already here kept their old text where those branches changed actions, expected results or records: for example 58 tests instead of 68 and 52 files instead of 53 in 0.8 and 1.2, 37 tests instead of 38 in 2.12, the revocation message of 4.14, the QR code of 4.6, and checks added to 2.13, 2.18, 3.6, 3.11, 5.2, 5.4 and 7.4 (M0 final review round 2: evidence R2-01, owner-readability R6-01, completeness R1-01). On 20 October 2026 every session was compared with the merged checklists again, change by change from `git diff 471b143 -- Docs/Stage0/device-checklists/`, and each change was carried in; Section 8 ends with the list. Two checks back this up (Section 9): `runbook-coverage.py` checks only that every checklist step is classified in Section 7 and that every blocking one has a session (step numbers; `evidence/M0-Integration/final-fix/runbook-coverage.log`), and `runbook-content-check.py` compares contents: every quoted text and count of a checklist step must appear in the runbook, or be listed in the check with its reason, and every quoted count or expected text of a runbook session must appear in its gate's checklist or be listed as one of this runbook's own corrections or additions (`evidence/M0-Integration/final-fix-2/runbook-content-check.log`). Neither check can show that a paraphrase means what the checklist means; if you find a difference, follow the checklist and note it in the session log.
- **Nothing in this runbook has been run on a device.** Every step stays **pending** until you run it and record the result. The agents that wrote it (14 October 2026, at M0 integration) only read the checklists, reports and harness code, built the harnesses for a generic iOS device and checked test filters on their own simulator; Section 9 lists those checks and their logs. Agent-written and agent-reviewed (the changes of 19 and 20 October 2026 not reviewed again); human review pending.
- **The corrections.** Section 8 lists 56 corrections this runbook made to the checklists, reports and ADRs as they stood at `471b143` (steps that could not be done as written, wrong expected results, missing and duplicated steps, and steps that an ADR and this runbook placed in different milestones), each with the exact text to change. M0 assembly applied them to those files, except the ones that wait for a decision (C28, C46's script change, C47, C49, C50, C51 and C56: integration log section 7.3); where one of those differs from its checklist, this runbook's version holds until its decision is taken, and Section 7 says what each changes. Every step here gives its actions, expected result and record; only the longest lists of expected values (G0.4 step 1.3's result tables, G0.5 AI step 9.2's strings) stay in the checklist, and the step names them.
- **Which steps block the M0 gate** and which can wait for a later milestone, with the reason for each: Section 7.

## 2. How to record results

For every step write: the date (UTC), the tester, the device model and identifier (for example iPhone18,1), its OS version and build, the commit you built from (`git rev-parse HEAD`), the build configuration (Debug or Release), the result and what you saw; for timing and transfer steps also the free storage (Settings > General > iPhone Storage) and whether the phone was charging or in Low Power Mode (plan 19.1 asks for the device and storage conditions); then attach the logs and screenshots the step names.

- **Results** (the same words in every gate, where the checklists use four different ones: correction C31): `passed-device` (the step's expected result, on that device); `failed` (anything else: write what happened instead, and never edit an expected result to match); `inconclusive` (only where a step allows it, such as G0.5 recovery step 3); `blocked` (a device, account or person is missing: say which). An inconclusive or blocked step leaves its report row pending-device or pending-human, with the reason.
- **Privacy:** never record a pairing code, token, invitation secret or API key, an IP address beyond its first two octets (in every gate, as G0.5 AI's checklist asks: `192.168.x.x`, `100.101.x.x`), the tailnet part of a MagicDNS name (`mac.[tailnet].ts.net`), personal data, or file names of your own files; redact (`[pairing code redacted]`) or crop. Session 4 gives the command that redacts addresses in a log. Screenshots as PNG under 2 MB; logs as plain text.
- **Then:** in the gate's report, change each row the step feeds from pending-device or pending-human to `passed-device` or `failed`, naming the device, OS build and evidence file (or leave that to an agent working from your records), and add one row per sitting to [`evidence/M0-runbook/sessions.md`](evidence/M0-runbook/sessions.md).

## 7. What blocks the M0 gate

**The rule.** A step **blocks M0** when (a) a gate criterion of plan 21.1, as the gate's report states it, needs it; (b) an ADR that the M0 exit approves lists it as pending device or human evidence, or as a device run in its open items, without putting it in a later milestone (an item asked of device A "when available" counts, because A must be at hand for G0.6 anyway); or (c) a decision or sign-off the M0 exit takes depends on it (plan Section 23, the release scope). Device confirmations of rows that passed only on the simulator block when (a), (b) or (c) applies: plan 19.1 and `CLAUDE.md` never let a simulator result stand for a device. A step **can wait** when (d) the plan, the gate's report, its checklist or its ADR puts it in a later milestone, or (e) no criterion, ADR item or decision needs it and a later milestone's acceptance produces the evidence; the gap is then recorded in the M0 gate summary with the milestone named.

Where this runbook proposes that a step an ADR lists for M0 can wait, it asks the ADR's owner for that change (corrections C47, C49, C50 and C56, Section 8) and says so below: **until the owner accepts the correction, the step blocks M0.** Your decisions D-1 (the iPad), D-2 (no device A in time), D-4 (live AI) and D-9 (the minimum OS) can also move blocking steps, each as an exception recorded where the decision says, and D-10 decides whether this rule is the M0 exit's at all (its option (i), recommended) or only the decisions' and sign-offs' inputs block the exit (option (ii)).
