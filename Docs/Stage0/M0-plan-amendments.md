## How to read this

**Plan exit rule.** Plan 21.1: "A failed spike changes the documented scope/approach before downstream work, not after the team has built around it." Rows failed in four gates on the simulator: G0.1 PDF's highlight overlay on text drawn with a rotated text matrix (row 10c, added to the report after `471b143`, so draft 0.5 missed it; A54), G0.2's Tailscale address forms without an App Transport Security exception (two rows), G0.4's start/resume budget for long audio files that stay on precise timing (rows 21 and 33), and G0.6's `UIFontMetrics` scaling (one row). Each failure is turned into an approach change below, stated as a proposal with its evidence, and approved by the product owner on 22 October 2026 (A54, A13 with D16, A22, A40); D16 holds the Tailscale route's form open until the security sign-off (Section 1.1).

**What this does not do.** No budget is relaxed. Every narrowing of release scope is a proposal. Six are forced by a failed or narrowed spike (Section 1.1). Six more, which no failed spike forces, are the product owner's decisions: the plan states each as the recommended default, beside the alternative that keeps the plan's scope, and approving draft 0.5 does not by itself approve any of them (Section 1.2). On 22 October 2026 the product owner approved the six forced narrowings and decided each of the other six as recommended. Section 1.3 lists the narrowings considered and not proposed. No ADR status, contract, fixture or code is changed. The Companion Vision addendum and the roadmap (plan Section 24, which draft 0.6 revised on main) are not touched by these amendments. Nothing is inferred for a physical device: every Stage 0 result is host or simulator, and every device and human step is still pending.

**Classifications** (one per finding, the main effect):

- *scope narrowing*: something the plan offered or targeted is excluded or made conditional, whether or not a failed spike forces it (Section 1 says which);
- *approach change*: how the plan says to build something changes;
- *budget change*: a Section 19.2 budget changes (none does);
- *value change*: a number, limit or token value the plan states or leaves open is set or changed;
- *new risk*: a risk the register did not have;
- *clarification*: the plan was silent, ambiguous or internally inconsistent, and the text now says what the evidence showed.

**Evidence paths** are relative to `Docs/Stage0/`: reports such as `G01-EPUB.md`, raw outputs under `evidence/<gate>/`. ADRs are in `Docs/ADRs/`. Every path in backticks below that names a report, starts with `evidence/` or `device-checklists/`, or starts with a top-level repository folder such as `Spikes/` or `Contracts/` is checked to exist by `evidence/M0-plan-amendments/commands/check-amendments.py` (its log is `evidence/M0-plan-amendments/check-amendments.log`).

**Decision owners.** "PO-nn" questions are the product owner's (Section 4). Questions for design, security, the technical lead, QA, the device run and others are in Section 5.

## 1. Scope changes

Each was a proposal; the product owner approved them on 22 October 2026, deciding each of Section 1.2's with its own question as recommended (Section 4). Every Section 2 row classified *scope narrowing* has its tag in 1.1 or 1.2, every tag below has a Section 2 row, and plan Appendix D names the same tags; the checker verifies all three.

### 1.1 Narrowings forced by a failed or narrowed spike

1. **Tailscale route (G0.2, failed rows).** With the pinned self-signed Mac identity and no ATS key, iOS refuses a Tailscale address and a MagicDNS name. Proposed: a scoped ATS exception (new decision D16, security review); if it is not approved, v1 offers no Tailscale route and XFER-04 narrows to LAN addresses, `.local` names and manually entered LAN addresses. [M0-A13]
2. **Opus in WebM (G0.4).** Not playable on iOS 26.5 (simulator); excluded from the codec list. FLAC, Opus (Ogg, MP4, CAF) and WAV join the release list only after the device run. [M0-A23]
3. **Long audio files on precise timing (G0.4, failed budget rows).** The budget is kept. Proposed: the per-file timing mode and the import remux; without the remux, native FLAC, MP3 without a header and very long Ogg Opus files are listed with their length limits and VBR MP3 with its depth limit. [M0-A22]
4. **EPUB compatibility subset (G0.1).** Fixed layout refused; a content document declared XHTML or HTML must be well-formed XHTML; an SVG or other XML document is shown only when nothing active is in it. The plan already said "reflowable" and "documented compatibility subset"; this documents the subset. [M0-A03]
5. **Quick capture (G0.4).** The narrowing plan 3.3 already proposed is confirmed by the evidence: the in-player Capture button is the release baseline; quick capture is not advertised until its device steps pass. [M0-A26]
6. **The `sylvia://` pairing link (G0.2 security finding R2-05).** Another installed app can claim the scheme and receive the pin and the invitation secret, which breaks the user-mediated channel plan 15.2 relies on, so the link is not advertised. Pairing is by the copied code or, optionally as in draft 0.4, a QR code scanned with Sylvia's own scanner. Keeping the link needs the security reviewer to accept the interception risk (PO-11). [M0-A16]

*(22 October 2026: approved by the product owner. D16 was decided as recommended (PO-07): the scoped exception if the security review approves it, otherwise no Tailscale route; the product owner gives that security sign-off personally, after G0.2 device steps 6 and 23, so which of the two holds is still pending. PO-11 (a): the link is dropped. PO-17 (a): in-player Capture is the baseline, quick capture shipped only after its device steps pass.)*

### 1.2 Narrowings no failed spike forces: the product owner's decisions

Each is stated in the plan as a pending decision, beside the alternative that keeps the plan's scope. Approving draft 0.5 does not by itself approve any of them: each needs the product owner's explicit choice in Section 4, and choosing the alternative named beside it keeps the plan's scope. *(22 October 2026: the product owner made each choice, every one as recommended: PO-05 (a), PO-36 (a), PO-31 (a) and PO-32, PO-30 (a) on its condition (G0.6 device step 19 shows the system's Reduce Motion behaviour acceptable, otherwise a custom presentation), PO-16 (c) and PO-27 (c).)*

1. **PDFs that forbid copying (G0.1 PDF finding 3; PO-05).** Earlier drafts were ambiguous for these files: 9.2's supported experience covers text-based PDFs, while "Unsupported/encrypted items show a specific error", and such a file is encrypted although it opens without a password. Proposed: open them for reading with no quotation, search or AI text, the conservative reading of 8.3 ("without attempting circumvention") and 18.2. Alternatives: every text feature, which keeps 9.2's experience, or personal quotation and search without AI text. [M0-A10]
2. **Cleartext to a local model server (ADR-0011 network policy; PO-36).** Proposed: cleartext only at IP literals in the loopback, private and link-local ranges, because a name can be spoofed or rebound; a server reached by `localhost`, a `.local` name, a tailnet address (100.64.0.0/10, also carrier-grade NAT space) or a MagicDNS name needs HTTPS. Alternative, which draft 0.4's tailnet paragraph ("explain any exception") left open: an explicit per-endpoint exception for such names and addresses (security review). [M0-A14]
3. **Bounded Dynamic Type exceptions (G0.6 findings 9, 14 and 15; PO-31, PO-32).** Proposed: bars stop growing at xxxLarge and use the large content viewer, as the system bars Sylvia replaces do; work, sheet and area titles step down, never below Large, until their longest word fits; the mini-player stops at xxxLarge or collapses to one row that keeps growing. Alternative that keeps principle 5's "Dynamic Type everywhere": bars and the mini-player keep growing (the mini-player took 54 % of an iPhone SE screen at AX5) and titles break inside a word. [M0-A40] [M0-A43]
4. **Reduce Motion for system sheets (G0.6 finding 8; PO-30).** Proposed, only if G0.6 device step 19 shows the system's Reduce Motion behavior acceptable: sheets presented by the system follow it, and T51's Reduce Motion clause covers Sylvia's own components. Alternative that keeps T51 as written: a custom sheet presentation. [M0-A42]
5. **EPUBs imported only as single files (G0.3 open question 3; PO-16).** In Stage 0 the share extension refused an EPUB offered as a folder (unpacked, as iCloud Drive or a Mac's Books library can keep one), with its own reason; earlier drafts did not restrict an EPUB's form. Proposed: in V1, EPUBs are imported only as single files, so a folder is refused that way on every route. Alternatives that keep the plan's scope: accept folders on the phone, behind safety checks of their own, or have the Mac companion pack them into `.epub` files. [M0-A53]
6. **Earlier turns after a change of provider or model (G0.5 AI F15; PO-27).** Earlier drafts asked for consent again when the destination changed (14.2), which kept the conversation going. Proposed: switching provider or model starts the new destination without the earlier turns, with a stated reason, so earlier turns, which may carry the user's notes, never go to a destination the user has not approved for them. Alternative that keeps the plan's scope: ask the user whether to resend them, with consent for the new destination. [M0-A34]

### 1.3 Narrowings considered and not proposed

1. **Quotations across EPUB chapters (G0.1 EPUB F8; PO-03).** One gesture cannot cross resources, but ordered `segment` anchors store such a quotation, so the capture flow keeps the cross-resource quotation earlier drafts provided for, through an action that extends a quotation into the next chapter. Keeping each selection within one resource would be a narrowing no failed spike forces; it is PO-03's alternative, not the proposal. [M0-A05]
2. **"Open With" from the Files app (G0.3 F6; PO-15).** With `LSSupportsOpeningDocumentsInPlace` NO the simulator's Files app lists no app under "Open With"; the proposal switches the key to YES if a phone does the same, so the route is kept either way. [M0-A20]
3. **Raw files copied with Finder (G0.3 F15; PO-37; draft 0.7).** Nothing proves a raw Finder copy complete, so a paused or stopped copy can be staged as it is. The proposal keeps the raw import, with that limit stated and a risk row, and steers large transfers to `.sylviapack`; accepting only `.sylviapack` from Finder above a size would be a narrowing no failed spike forces, and it is PO-37's alternative, not the proposal. [M0-A20]

*(22 October 2026: the product owner decided all three as proposed: PO-03 (a), PO-15 (a) until G0.3 device step 13, and PO-37 (a) and (c).)*
