# Device checklist: G0.1 PDF (PDFKit)

Status of every step: **pending** until run on a physical device. Nothing here has been inferred from the simulator.

## Setup

1. On the build Mac, from the repository root: `source Scripts/lib/env.sh; source Scripts/lib/xcodegen.sh; sylvia_generate Spikes/G01-PDF`.
2. Open `Spikes/G01-PDF/G01PDF.xcodeproj`, select the `PDFSpikeHarness` scheme, set the signing team to `A4KBDX665Z` for the `PDFSpikeHarness` and `PDFAnchorKit` targets (do not commit the change; the project is regenerated), choose the device and Run.
3. Record in `Docs/Stage0/evidence/G01-PDF/device-run.md`: device model, iOS version and build (Settings > General > About), Xcode build, the commit hash, date, and the tester's name. Screenshots go next to it as `device-<step>.png` (under 2 MB each; crop if needed).
4. Devices: the iPhone 17 Pro (iPhone18,1, iOS 26.7) for every step; repeat steps 1 and 10 on device A, the small-screen baseline (step 1 there answers report open question 3 for device A). *(22 October 2026: the oldest supported iPhone is the product owner's iPhone 11 Pro, decision D-1 (b); until then this line named the iPhone 12-class baseline. The harness opens `labels-multipage.pdf`. Use the **Open** menu (top left) to switch fixtures. The bottom lines show the page index and printed label, highlight status, open timing, link decisions and any URL the system delivered to the harness. Below them are eight invisible one-point labels for the UI tests: link, overlay and selection frames, two probe points and the view frame, and, for the bundled fixtures only, the values of the text form fields. They carry no page text, but VoiceOver will reach them (step 6). **Show** scrolls to the first highlight; **Zoom** sets 2.5 x the fit scale and scrolls to it.

On the simulator, `SelectionGestureUITests` already drags the end selection handle across three lines, from the left column into the right one, and into the second line on each rotated page of `rotated.pdf` (report row 2a). On the unrotated pages, downward drags left the selection end 19 to 29 pt (two to three lines at fit zoom) above the touch point, so that test needed corrective drags; on the rotated pages one drag was enough each time. Simulated touches are not a finger: note in steps 1-3 whether a finger needs corrections.

## Steps

1. **Finger selection across lines.** Open `labels-multipage.pdf`, scroll to page index 3 (printed label 1). Long-press a word in the first body line, drag the end handle down to the middle of the third line, then choose **Highlight** in the edit menu.
   - Expected: the status line reads "Saved highlight (n on this file)"; the selection is dismissed and, at once (no flash, no visible delay), an Ochre fill with a darker Ochre underline appears on exactly the selected words on all three lines and nowhere else.
   - Record: screenshot `device-1.png`; passed-device or failed, any mismatch, whether the handle landed where your finger aimed, and whether the highlight appeared at once.
   - Then make two selections that end at the end of a line and highlight each: long-press the last word of the first body line ("water", the end of "The pond in winter ... the water"), and select the whole third body line ("who stops at the shore hears it settle.") by dragging its end handle just past the line's last word. Record whether the handle stayed on that line: on the simulator, a drag ending 6 pt past a line's end selected to the line's end, while one ending 40 pt past a left-column line of `two-column.pdf` selected part of the title above instead (report row 8a). (Before the line-end fix such a selection was saved but could not be restored; report finding 11.) Step 4 checks that both come back after a relaunch.
2. **Selection across columns.** Open `two-column.pdf`. Start a selection in the last line of the left column ("row like the spines of closed books.") and drag the handle into the first two lines of the right column. Highlight.
   - Expected: the selection follows reading order (end of left column, then top of right column) without picking up unrelated lines; the overlay covers the same words.
   - Record: screenshot `device-2.png`; describe any lines the handle could not reach or any extra lines selected.
3. **Rotated pages.** Open `rotated.pdf`. On each of page indices 1 (90), 2 (180), 3 (270) and 5 (270 with crop), select two lines with a finger and Highlight. (The simulator made the same selections with simulated touches, report row 2a; this step is a person's finger and eye.)
   - Expected: the selection handles move along the rotated text; each overlay sits on the selected words, underline on the baseline side of the text.
   - Record: one screenshot per page `device-3-p<index>.png`; passed-device or failed per page; whether the handle landed where your finger aimed.
4. **Relaunch.** Force-quit the harness (app switcher), relaunch, open each file used above.
   - Expected: the status line reads "Highlights: n stored, n restored exactly" with the same n as saved (on `labels-multipage.pdf` this includes the two selections that end at a line's end); overlays reappear in the same places.
   - Record: screenshot `device-4.png` and the status text.
5. **Zoom.** On `rotated.pdf` page index 1, pinch to about 400 % and pan across a highlight; then rotate the device to landscape and back. (The simulator measured the overlay's colours on screen at 2.5 x zoom and in both orientations, report row 11a; this step is the person's judgement of alignment at higher zoom with real gestures.)
   - Expected: the overlay stays aligned with the words at every zoom level and orientation (tiles redraw sharp after a moment); no offset grows with zoom.
   - Record: screenshots at about 100 % and 400 % (`device-5a.png`, `device-5b.png`), plus landscape (`device-5c.png`).
6. **VoiceOver reaches selection actions.** Turn on VoiceOver (Settings > Accessibility > VoiceOver). On `labels-multipage.pdf`, use the rotor or a double-tap-and-hold to select text, then find the selection actions.
   - Expected: VoiceOver reads the page text; a selection can be made; the **Highlight** action is reachable either in the edit menu or through the toolbar button announced "Highlight selection"; after activating it, the status line ("Saved highlight ...") is announced or reachable.
   - Record: which method worked (edit menu, toolbar, both, neither), exact VoiceOver announcements, and a screen recording if practical (`device-6.mov`, under 2 MB or describe instead). Note the invisible test-geometry labels VoiceOver reads (harness only, not shipped) but do not count them as a failure of the reader.
7. **Image-only pages.** Open `mixed-scanned.pdf`, go to page index 1 and try to select text.
   - Expected: nothing is selectable; the page line shows "No selectable text on this page"; Highlight gives "No selectable text on this page" or "Select text first", never a saved highlight.
   - Record: screenshot `device-7.png`.
8. **Links.** Open `actions.pdf`. Tap each of the four links once.
   - Expected: link A and link C (launch) do nothing visible except the "Links:" line showing "blocked"; link B shows "Open link?" with `https://example.invalid/track` (tap Cancel; Safari must not open); link D moves to page index 1; "URLs opened into the harness: none" throughout; no other app opens at any point, including when the file is first opened.
   - Record: the "Links:" and "URLs opened" lines; passed-device or failed.
   - Then open `actions-nonurl.pdf` and tap links A to I in order, returning to page index 0 (scroll up) after each. Expected: A goes to page index 1 and B to page index 2; C (Find), D (GoToPage) and F (another file) show "blocked" on the "Links:" line and stay on page 0; E (Print), H (JavaScript) and I (submit form) do nothing, and no print sheet or other panel appears; G resets the field below the links to "Default"; "URLs opened into the harness: none" throughout and no other app opens. Record the "Links:" line and anything that differs.
   - Then open `actions-chained.pdf` (every URI and JavaScript action in it aims at the harness's canary address except link D's https link: chained /Next actions, actions in /AA, form field actions; its open action and page 0's open trigger are GoTo actions to page index 2). Expected on opening: the page line reads "Page index 0 of 3" and stays so (the harness scrolls to page 0 itself, so watch for a jump to page index 2 in the first seconds). Tap links A to L in order, returning to page index 0 (scroll up) after each. Expected: A, B, C and G go to page index 1 and F to page index 2, each with nothing added to the "Links:" line; D shows "Open link?" with `https://example.invalid/chained` (tap Cancel); E, K and L each add "blocked" to the "Links:" line and stay on page 0; H, I and J do nothing at all. Then tap each of the three fields below the links, type a few characters, and tap the page outside the field: the field shows what you typed (autocorrection may change a word; record what it shows) and nothing is added to the "Links:" line. Then open another file from the Open menu: nothing is added to the "Links:" line. "URLs opened into the harness: none" throughout and no other app opens. Record the "Links:" line, the three fields' text and anything that differs. (The simulator test `LinkTapUITests` makes the same taps, typing a digit into each field; this step repeats them with a finger on a device.)
   - If an iPad with a trackpad or mouse is available: open `actions-chained.pdf`, move the pointer onto link F, onto link K and onto each field and away again without clicking, then click each field and click elsewhere. This reaches the mouse-enter and mouse-exit triggers, which no simulator test can (XCUITest cannot hover on iOS). Expected: moving the pointer and clicking the fields add nothing to the "Links:" line; "URLs opened into the harness: none"; no other app opens. Record the iPad model, iPadOS version and result; if no such iPad is available, record "not run: no iPad with a pointer".
9. **Protected and damaged files.** Open `encrypted-user-password.pdf` and `truncated.pdf` from the Open menu.
   - Expected: "This PDF is password-protected. Password-protected PDFs are not supported." and "This PDF is incomplete: the file ends early."; no password prompt; no crash.
   - Record: the two messages.
10. **Open time for a large PDF.** Build the harness in Release and install it without Xcode (plan 19.1): `xcodebuild build -project Spikes/G01-PDF/G01PDF.xcodeproj -scheme PDFSpikeHarness -configuration Release -destination "id=<device UDID>" -derivedDataPath Spikes/G01-PDF/.build/DerivedData-device-release -disableAutomaticPackageResolution -allowProvisioningUpdates -allowProvisioningDeviceRegistration DEVELOPMENT_TEAM=A4KBDX665Z`, then `xcrun devicectl device install app --device <device UDID> Spikes/G01-PDF/.build/DerivedData-device-release/Build/Products/Release-iphoneos/PDFSpikeHarness.app`, and launch it from the Home Screen. On the Mac, run `Scripts/fixtures/generate-g01-pdf-large.sh`, then AirDrop `$SYLVIA_FIXTURE_CACHE/generated/g01-pdf/large-1000.pdf` (1,000 pages, 1,713,026 bytes, SHA-256 `cfa95b03…2ee299`) to the device and save it in Files. Force-quit and relaunch the harness, then choose **Open > Open from Files…** and pick the file. Repeat five times (force-quit between runs so each open is cold for the app).
    - Expected (plan 19.2 budget for a typical file, p95 at or under 2 s): the timing line reads "Open … ms (digest, checks, parse); text of 1000 pages … ms; restore highlights … ms (n restored); first page drawn … ms after display began; total … ms". The budget applies to "open" plus "restore highlights" plus "first page drawn" (restoring stored highlights runs before display on every open; it touches only pages that carry highlights). The all-pages text extraction is the harness doing indexing synchronously and is recorded separately (the product indexes in the background).
    - Record: the five timing lines verbatim, the device model, and whether it is the oldest supported iPhone (an A13 phone: the iPhone 11, 11 Pro or 11 Pro Max, or the iPhone SE (2nd generation)) or a newer device (a newer device's result, device A's on the iPhone 13 mini included, does not stand in for the oldest supported iPhone). (Until 24 October 2026 the parenthesis named the iPhone 11 Pro, device A from 22 October 2026, decision D-1 (b), as the oldest supported iPhone, and the iPhone 12-class baseline before it.) Then select a word on any page, Highlight it, force-quit, and open the file from Files again five more times; record those timing lines too (they include restoring one highlight).

11. **Rotated text and stream order.** Open `text-layout.pdf`. On page index 0, select one of the vertical lines ("Upward line one ...") with a finger and Highlight it; do the same for an upside-down line. Then on page index 1 drag from the left column's last line into the right column's first line and Highlight. Force-quit, relaunch and open the file again.
    - Expected: the selections can be made and saved; after the relaunch the status line reads "Highlights: 3 stored, 3 restored exactly". The overlay on the rotated lines is known to be wrong (report row 10c and finding 12): the fill covers the line, but the underline is a block at one end of a vertical line and runs along the tops of the glyphs on an upside-down line. On page index 1 the selection follows reading order (the end of the left column, then the top of the right column).
    - Record: screenshots `device-11a.png` (page 0 with both highlights) and `device-11b.png` (page 1); whether a finger can select along a vertical line at all, and how the handles behave; the status text after the relaunch.

## Notes

- Steps 1-3 and 5 are judgements of on-screen alignment by a person; record what you see rather than "looks fine".
- If any step fails, record the failure as found; do not retry until it passes without noting the earlier failure.
