# Large content viewer on the tab bar at AX5

Environment: simulator (iPhone 17; model iPhone18,3, iOS 26.5), text size AccessibilityXXXL (launch argument). Generated by `AreaNavigationTests.testLargeContentViewerOnTabBarItem`. The Inbox tab bar item was held for 6 s with XCUITest `press(forDuration:)`; the host took full-screen screenshots with `xcrun simctl io <udid> screenshot` (scaled to 1x with `sips`) before the press and 2.5 s into it.

| Comparison (middle 60% x 40% of the screen) | Pixels changed by more than 24 levels | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| baseline-1 vs baseline-2 (no press; control) | 0.0000 | below 0.01 |
| baseline-1 vs inbox-long-press | 0.1089 | above 0.04 |

| Lift (required, as on a system tab bar) | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Inbox held 6 s, finger lifted on Inbox | Inbox selected; other items selected: none |
| Thoughts held 3 s, slid to Library, lifted | Library selected; other items selected: none |

Screenshots: `screens/iphone/lcv/baseline-1.png`, `baseline-2.png`, `inbox-long-press.png`. That the overlay shows the item's glyph and label legibly is judged from the screenshot, and on a device in checklist step 12.
