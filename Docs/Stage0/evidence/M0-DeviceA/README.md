## The product owner's decision of 24 October 2026

As the workflow relayed it to this update, verbatim:

- The owner wrote: "iphone 11 pro seems to be no longer charging. i do have a 13 mini that is still working, so update the plan. even better from a screen size."
- The orchestrating session replied that the 13 mini lays out at the same 375 × 812 points as the 11 Pro, so it is equal for layout rather than better, and physically smaller; and that its A15 chip is two generations newer than the A13, the slowest chip iOS 26 supports (the iPhone 11 family and the iPhone SE (2nd generation)), so speed and memory figures measured on it are optimistic for A13 phones. It asked: "With the iPhone 11 Pro out, how should device A (the oldest-supported-iPhone checks, runbook Session 8, about 7 h) be covered?"
- The options, verbatim:
  - "13 mini only (Recommended)": "The 13 mini becomes device A. The records say plainly that the slowest supported chip (A13) isn't measured on a device. That's left for M8's release device matrix, with a borrowed or bought A13 phone if wanted. Simplest, and no extra session time."
  - "iPhone 11 + 13 mini": "If the iPhone 11 still works: it becomes device A for the speed and memory checks (A13, the true floor), and the 13 mini covers the small-screen layout checks. Most rigorous, but adds roughly 2-3 hours of device time across the two phones."
  - "iPhone 11 only": "The iPhone 11 (A13) becomes device A. The speed floor is exact, but its 414-point screen is wider, so the 375-point small-screen layout floor stays a simulator check."
- The owner chose "13 mini only (Recommended)". Whether the iPhone 11 still works was not asked. The session later told the owner that the 13 mini draws each point about 8% smaller than the 11 Pro (fact 5), so the owner's legibility judgments on it are slightly stricter, which suits a small-screen check.

So decision D-1, part (i), was taken again by the owner on 24 October 2026; the records record it as a dated addendum to D-1 of 22 October 2026, not as a new reading of it. The option the owner chose leaves the A13 floor for M8's release device matrix "with a borrowed or bought A13 phone if wanted": the records say that the Section 19.2 budgets are to be measured there on an A13 iPhone, the owner's iPhone 11 if it still works then, or one borrowed or bought if the owner wants one, and that until an A13 iPhone has been measured no record says a budget holds on one.

## What changes and what does not

- **Device A is the product owner's iPhone 13 mini** from 24 October 2026: the baseline for small-screen layout on a device, not the oldest supported iPhone. The iPhone 11 Pro, device A under decision D-1 (b) of 22 October 2026, no longer charges and is not used. The iPhone 11 is not device A; it stays what the runbook says for phone D (any phone that can call phone B), and any result ever taken on it is recorded under its own model.
- **The steps that depend on the screen are covered as fully as on the iPhone 11 Pro**: layout, the type roles at the default and the largest accessibility sizes, and the design steps the owner judges (runbook 8.1-8.5; G0.6 steps 4, 13-15, 22-27, 29 and 30 on A). The same 375 × 812 pt, drawn about 8% smaller (facts 4 and 5). G0.6's criterion, the type roles "on the oldest supported iPhone" (plan 21.1), is checked on device A, whose screen in points is the iPhone 11 Pro's: among the oldest iPhones iOS 26 supports, only the iPhone SE (2nd generation) has a smaller one, 375 × 667 pt, which stays a simulator check, as before.
- **The figures that depend on the chip, the memory, heat or the storage are the iPhone 13 mini's (A15), never the A13 floor's.** From the checklists' own text, the steps on device A that record such a figure or judgment: G0.2 step 11 (the peak memory, the total time, the `milliseconds` of `assembly_verified` and `staging_checked`, `import_ms`, the largest `commit_ms`, any hang); G0.5 recovery steps 1 and 2 (the save latency, p95 of each run, which is ADR-0003's figure for the durability setting's cost; the protection classes, the SQLite version and the pragma readback depend on the iOS build, not the chip, and are covered); and G0.1 PDF step 1 (that a new highlight shows "at once (no flash, no visible delay)", a judgment of speed). G0.4 steps 8-10, 12 and 13 record no timing or memory figure: their outcomes are what iOS does with the intent while the phone is locked, and the only speed-dependent observation is the side of the start-up race step 12.2 records (`loaded=`), which is the iPhone 13 mini's. The G0.6 steps record none (step 29's five seconds of "Saving…" are the prototype's own slow write). The repeats on A that can wait for later milestones (G0.1 EPUB 11 and PDF 10, G0.2 22, G0.3's repeats, G0.4 1, 3 and 15) would also give the iPhone 13 mini's figures. No budget changes (plan 19.2): a miss on the iPhone 13 mini is a failure, recorded; a pass does not show that the budget holds on an A13 phone.
- **The A13 floor is not measured on a device in M0.** It is left for M8's release device matrix (plan 19.1 and 21.2's M8 row): the Section 19.2 budgets are to be measured there on an A13 iPhone, the owner's iPhone 11 if it still works then, or one borrowed or bought if the owner wants one.
- **D-2 (a) stands**: Session 8 runs on device A before the M0 exit, and the exit waits for it. But the chip-dependent results on A are not A13 results: a narrower form of the exception D-2's option (b) described, recorded where (b) said an exception goes, each as far as it applies: ADR-0001 (the whole decision) and ADR-0013 (it does not apply to G0.6's steps on A, which depend on the screen); ADR-0003 (the save latency), ADR-0005 (the highlight redraw), ADR-0007 (nothing but step 12's start-up race), ADR-0009 (step 11's memory, times, part commits and hangs) and ADR-0010 (the share extension's memory limit in G0.3's repeats, in M2); and the G0.2 report's row T38.
- **Unchanged:** phone B, the iPad, every budget value, the release scope, every other decision, Session 8's steps and their time estimate (the runbook's), and the status of every report row: every device step stays pending until it runs.
