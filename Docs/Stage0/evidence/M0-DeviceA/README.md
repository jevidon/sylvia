## The product owner's decision of 24 October 2026

As the workflow relayed it to this update, verbatim:

- The owner wrote: "iphone 11 pro seems to be no longer charging. i do have a 13 mini that is still working, so update the plan. even better from a screen size."
- The orchestrating session replied that the 13 mini lays out at the same 375 × 812 points as the 11 Pro, so it is equal for layout rather than better, and physically smaller; and that its A15 chip is two generations newer than the A13, the slowest chip iOS 26 supports (the iPhone 11 family and the iPhone SE (2nd generation)), so speed and memory figures measured on it are optimistic for A13 phones. It asked: "With the iPhone 11 Pro out, how should device A (the oldest-supported-iPhone checks, runbook Session 8, about 7 h) be covered?"
- The options, verbatim:
  - "13 mini only (Recommended)": "The 13 mini becomes device A. The records say plainly that the slowest supported chip (A13) isn't measured on a device. That's left for M8's release device matrix, with a borrowed or bought A13 phone if wanted. Simplest, and no extra session time."
  - "iPhone 11 + 13 mini": "If the iPhone 11 still works: it becomes device A for the speed and memory checks (A13, the true floor), and the 13 mini covers the small-screen layout checks. Most rigorous, but adds roughly 2-3 hours of device time across the two phones."
  - "iPhone 11 only": "The iPhone 11 (A13) becomes device A. The speed floor is exact, but its 414-point screen is wider, so the 375-point small-screen layout floor stays a simulator check."
- The owner chose "13 mini only (Recommended)". Whether the iPhone 11 still works was not asked. The session later told the owner that the 13 mini draws each point about 8% smaller than the 11 Pro (fact 5), so the owner's legibility judgments on it are slightly stricter, which suits a small-screen check.

So decision D-1, part (i), was taken again by the owner on 24 October 2026; the records record it as a dated addendum to D-1 of 22 October 2026, not as a new reading of it. The option the owner chose leaves the A13 floor for M8's release device matrix "with a borrowed or bought A13 phone if wanted": the records say that the Section 19.2 budgets are to be measured there on an A13 iPhone, the owner's iPhone 11 if it still works then, or one borrowed or bought if the owner wants one, and that until an A13 iPhone has been measured no record says a budget holds on one.
