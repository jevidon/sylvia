## The product owner's decision of 24 October 2026 (device A)

The product owner (Justin Evidon, product owner and technical lead) wrote: "iphone 11 pro seems to be no longer
charging. i do have a 13 mini that is still working, so update the plan. even better from a screen size."

The orchestrating session replied that the 13 mini lays out at the same 375 × 812 points as the 11 Pro, so it is equal
for layout rather than better, and physically smaller; and that its A15 chip is two generations newer than the A13, the
slowest chip iOS 26 supports (the iPhone 11 family and the iPhone SE (2nd generation)), so speed and memory figures
measured on it are optimistic for A13 phones. It asked: "With the iPhone 11 Pro out, how should device A (the
oldest-supported-iPhone checks, runbook Session 8, about 7 h) be covered?" The options, verbatim:

- "13 mini only (Recommended)": "The 13 mini becomes device A. The records say plainly that the slowest supported chip
  (A13) isn't measured on a device. That's left for M8's release device matrix, with a borrowed or bought A13 phone if
  wanted. Simplest, and no extra session time."
- "iPhone 11 + 13 mini": "If the iPhone 11 still works: it becomes device A for the speed and memory checks (A13, the
  true floor), and the 13 mini covers the small-screen layout checks. Most rigorous, but adds roughly 2-3 hours of
  device time across the two phones."
- "iPhone 11 only": "The iPhone 11 (A13) becomes device A. The speed floor is exact, but its 414-point screen is wider,
  so the 375-point small-screen layout floor stays a simulator check."

The owner chose "13 mini only (Recommended)". Whether the iPhone 11 still works was not asked. The session later told
the owner that the 13 mini draws each point about 8% smaller than the 11 Pro, so the owner's legibility judgments on it
are slightly stricter, which suits a small-screen check.

What the decision means, as relayed: device A is the owner's iPhone 13 mini from 24 October 2026; the iPhone 11 Pro,
device A under decision D-1 (b) of 22 October 2026, no longer charges and is not used; the iPhone 11 is not device A,
stays what the runbook says for phone D (any phone that can call phone B), and any result ever taken on it is recorded
under its own model. D-1 was decided again by the owner on 24 October 2026 (not a new reading of the old decision). The
screen-dependent steps on A are covered on the 13 mini as fully as on the 11 Pro; the chip-, memory-, thermal- and
storage-dependent figures on A are A15 results, recorded as the 13 mini's, never as the A13 floor's, with no budget
changed (plan 19.2). The A13 floor is not measured on a device in M0: it moves to M8 (plan 21.2's M8 row), where, before
release, the Section 19.2 budgets are measured on an A13 iPhone, the owner's iPhone 11 if it still works then, or a
borrowed or bought one. This is a narrower form of decision D-2's option (b): D-2 (a) stands, Session 8 runs on device
A before the M0 exit and the exit waits for it, but the chip-dependent results on A are not A13 results. Nothing here is
a device result: no 13 mini has been registered or run; Session 0.4 records its model identifier, iOS version and build.

## The device facts, as relayed, and what this agent checked again

1. Apple, "iPhone 13 mini - Technical Specifications" (https://support.apple.com/en-nz/111873): a 5.4-inch all-screen
   OLED display, 2340 by 1080 pixels at 476 ppi; the A15 Bionic chip; a Lightning connector; Face ID; a Ring/Silent
   switch and no Action button.
2. Apple, "iPhone 11 Pro - Technical Specifications" (https://support.apple.com/en-nz/111879): a 5.8-inch display, 2436
   by 1125 pixels at 458 ppi; the A13 Bionic chip; a Lightning connector.
3. Xcode 27.1's simulator device types: "iPhone 13 mini" has the model identifier iPhone14,4 ("iPhone 11 Pro"
   iPhone12,3, "iPhone 11" iPhone12,1, "iPhone SE (2nd generation)" iPhone12,8), and a 1080 × 2340-pixel main screen at
   scale 3 and 476 ppi. Until Session 0.4 reads the phone, the records write "iPhone14,4 expected".
4. Layout size, to confirm on the simulator: the 13 mini lays out at 375 × 812 points, as the 11 Pro does; iOS renders at
   scale 3 and the panel shows 1080 pixels across 375 points, a native scale of 2.88.
5. Physical size: a 375-point line spans 1125 px / 458 ppi = 2.456 in on the 11 Pro and 1080 px / 476 ppi = 2.269 in on
   the 13 mini, a ratio of about 0.924: the 13 mini draws everything about 7.6% smaller.
6. Apple's iPhone User Guide for iOS 26 (https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/iphone-models-compatible-with-ios-26-iphe3fa5df43/26/ios/26)
   lists the iPhone 13 mini; its oldest models are the iPhone 11 family and the iPhone SE (2nd generation), all A13.
7. Apple does not publish either phone's RAM, so the records state none; a memory figure taken on the 13 mini is the
   13 mini's, never an A13 phone's.
